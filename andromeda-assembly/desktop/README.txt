Andromeda Insights

Thank you for choosing an Andromeda Insights PC.

It is the year 2291. You're the newest builder aboard Forge-7, Andromeda
Insights' orbital foundry above Titan. Build machines by hand, put them through
QA, pack them, and launch them to colonies across the Sol system. Every flawless
build helps decode a signal from the Andromeda Galaxy.


GETTING STARTED
  1. Extract this folder anywhere, e.g. your Desktop or C:\Games.
  2. Double-click "Andromeda Assembly.exe".
  3. Optional: right-click the exe and choose "Pin to Start" or "Pin to taskbar".

  No installation, account or internet connection is needed.


CONTROLS
  Mouse          Drag parts, hold to torque screws and apply paste, drag cables
  H              Hint for the next step
  Esc            Pause
  M              Mute
  F              Fullscreen
  Right-drag     Look around   (mouse wheel zooms)


SAVES & SETTINGS
  Progress is saved automatically to:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Andromeda Insights\Andromeda Assembly
  Graphics quality starts at Ultra and lowers itself on slower PCs. You can
  change it any time in Settings.


SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with Microsoft Edge (preinstalled) or Google Chrome.
  Any GPU with WebGL 2. A dedicated GPU is recommended for Ultra.


UNINSTALLING
  Delete this folder. To also remove saves, delete the folder listed under
  "Saves & settings".


TROUBLESHOOTING
  Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time you
  run the app. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  If the screen stays black, open Settings in the game and lower the quality.
