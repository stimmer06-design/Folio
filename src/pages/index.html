The invoice

A document, not a dashboard export.

Your client opens the PDF and sees a properly typeset invoice: clear hierarchy, aligned figures, and nothing that isn’t needed.

  1. Your name, your logo

    Top left, where a letterhead puts it. Folio never adds its own branding.

  2. Amount due, stated once

    Set large beside the due date and terms, so nobody has to look for it.

  3. Figures that line up

    Tabular numerals and hairline rules keep quantities, prices and totals in clean columns.

  4. How to pay you

    Bank details or a payment link sit at the foot of the page, where clients expect them.
