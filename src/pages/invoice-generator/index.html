A form on one side, the invoice on the other.

The generator is a single page. You type into six short sections; the finished invoice is redrawn beside them at its real proportions, with every total worked out. When it looks right, you download it or print it.

  • Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for
  • Saved in your browser as you type, so you can close the tab
  • On a phone, Edit and Preview become two tabs

Every field

What to put in each section.

Only a few fields are needed for a valid invoice. The rest are there when your work calls for them. For the reasoning behind each, read what to put on an invoice.

01 · Your business
Name and logoYour trading name as clients know it. The logo is optional and sits top left.
Contact detailsEmail, phone and address, so the client knows who to ask about the invoice.
Tax IDYour VAT, GST or tax registration number, if you have one and your clients need it.
02 · Client
NameThe person or company responsible for paying, which is not always the person you worked with.
Email and addressWhere the invoice goes, and the billing address their accounts team will expect.
03 · Invoice details
NumberSuggested for you, one higher than your last. See how to number invoices.
Dates and termsThe issue date and payment terms. Choose Net 30 and the due date is set for you.
Currency and paperSixteen currencies; US Letter or A4.
04 · Line items
DescriptionWhat you did or supplied, specific enough that the client recognizes it.
Quantity and priceHours and an hourly rate, units and a unit price, or 1 and a fixed fee.
05 · Tax, discount & shipping
TaxYour rate and its name (sales tax, VAT, GST), applied after any discount.
DiscountA percentage or a fixed amount off the subtotal.
Other chargeShipping, travel or a call-out fee, added after tax.
DepositAnything already paid, subtracted to show the balance due.
06 · Payment & notes
PaymentHow to pay you: bank details, a payment link or who to make a check out to.
NotesAnything else: a thank-you, a warranty, a late-payment term you agreed in advance.
