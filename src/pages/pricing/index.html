Questions

Will the free plan stay free?

Yes. Creating, downloading and printing invoices will always be free and unlimited. Pro pays for the parts that need servers: sync, client lists and recurring invoices.

When will Pro be available?

It is in development. Join the early-access list and we will write to you once, when it is ready, with an early-access price.

Will my free invoices move to Pro?

Yes. Pro will import the invoices saved in your browser, so nothing you make today is lost.
