How they work

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The tools run in your browser, like the generator, and none of them guesses a tax rate for you.

The same arithmetic

The tax calculator uses the generator’s own code, so its totals are the totals your invoice will show.

Straight into an invoice

Each tool opens a new invoice with its numbers filled in. Your saved invoices are left as they are.

Your rate, not ours

Tax rates depend on where and what you sell, and they change. You enter the rate; the tools do the sums.
